1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Magic Paint: Kids Coloring & Drawing Book ("we", "our", or "the App", Package ID: com.magicpaint.kidscoloring). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information handled when children and parents use our mobile application.

Our app is designed specifically for children of all ages, toddlers, preschoolers, and families. We adhere strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), the Google Play Families Policy, and the Apple App Store Kids Category Guidelines.

🔍

2. What Data We Handle (Data Summary)

We believe in complete transparency. Here is an exact summary of data types and how they are handled:

Data Type Collected? Stored On Purpose
Child's Name / Identity No Local Only Optional display on printable certificates & book cover
Email / Contact Details No None Not required or requested
Drawings & Masterpieces No Local Storage Save artwork progress and sticker albums
Device Location / GPS No None Never accessed or used
Microphone / Audio Recording No None Never recorded (TTS text-to-speech is playback only)

📱

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The App may request minimal device permissions solely to support core creative features:

  • Photo Gallery / Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Gal): Used solely when the user taps "Save to Gallery" to export a finished coloring masterpiece or certificate into the device's native photo album. No images are uploaded to any server.
  • Camera (Optional AR Stencil Mode): Used exclusively in real-time on the device to project an outline stencil on paper for tracing. No photos or video frames are stored, processed externally, or transmitted.
  • Audio Output (Speaker): Used to play whimsical cheerful background melodies, brush sounds, and spoken color names via on-device Text-to-Speech (TTS).
🖨️

4. Printable Coloring Book & PDF Export

Our app allows parents to generate high-resolution PDF coloring books, crayon guides, and completion certificates.

💡 100% Client-Side PDF Generation: All PDF documents are compiled entirely inside the application's local sandbox on your device using Dart vector libraries. No PDF data or child personalization text is ever sent across the internet.
🛡️

5. Child Safety & COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance

We are fully committed to creating a safe and wholesome environment for children:

  • No Behavioral Advertising: We do not serve interest-based or behavioral ads to children.
  • No In-App Social Media Links or Chat: There are no public chat rooms, social media feeds, or direct messaging features where children could interact with strangers.
  • No Account Registration: Children can dive straight into coloring without signing up or creating an account.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

6. Parental Rights & Data Management

Parents and legal guardians have absolute control over their child's data at all times:

  • You can delete all saved artworks and quest achievements at any time by clearing the app's cache or uninstalling the app.
  • You can revoke photo or camera permissions at any time through your device's System Settings.
  • If you have any questions or wish to request information, you may contact our dedicated child privacy support team.
